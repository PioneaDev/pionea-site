Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026. Applies to pioneagames.com and the game "Colony 2060: Space Clicker".

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Virieux Consulting, operating the Pionea Games brand. Contact: contact@pioneagames.com.

2. What we do not collect

The game does not ask for an account, e-mail, name, or access to your contacts, photos or location. Your progress is saved only on your device. The nickname you enter in the game stays on your device.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads, including rewarded ads, provided by Google AdMob. In that case Google and its partners may collect technical data: the device advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version and interactions with ads. This data is used to serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message is shown before any personalized advertising; you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads). See Google's privacy policy.

4. In-app purchases

The game offers in-app purchases, processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment methods or billing details.

5. Technical data

Google Play may provide us with anonymized statistics (downloads, crashes) in the developer console. We use them to fix bugs and improve the game.

6. Website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics tool. The host may keep technical logs (IP address) under its own policy.

7. Children

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability regarding your data. Write to contact@pioneagames.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

To erase the game's local data, uninstall it or clear its data in Android settings.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
