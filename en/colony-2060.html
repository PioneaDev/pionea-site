Colony 2060: Space Clicker

Build a colony on a distant planet: construct, explore, upgrade.

Coming soon on Google Play

What you do

Build
Place mines on deposits and link them to your base with roads.
Explore
Uncover the map tile by tile. The radar and the chest detector reveal deposits and treasure.
Produce and sell
Your mines produce resources. Sell them at the market for Cosmos.
Upgrade
Level up the laboratory to unlock new research: oxygen, production, terracing.
Achieve
More than sixty achievements to unlock, with diamonds as rewards.
Get guided
NOA, the colony's assistant, walks you through your first minutes.
Play in your language
The game is translated into 16 languages.
